From metadata to a model score

A reproducible synthetic dataset contains ordinary purchases, legitimate unusual behavior, and fraudulent purchases with overlapping patterns.

Three classifiers are compared: Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and Gradient Boosting. Preprocessing and sigmoid calibration happen inside cross-validation. Model and threshold selection use validation F1; an untouched test split provides the final evaluation.

Understand the limits

  • Generated patterns are simpler than real fraud.
  • False positives and missed fraud remain possible.
  • Calibration on synthetic data does not establish real financial probabilities.
  • The model does not use card credentials, customer identity, or actual bank data.
  • A score or flag is not a financial decision or a confirmed fraud finding.
