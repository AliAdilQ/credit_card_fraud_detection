# Code of conduct

We welcome contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, patient, and constructive. Discuss the work, listen to feedback, and give credit for others' contributions.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sharing private information, and abusive behavior are not accepted. Use synthetic examples; never disclose another person's financial data or credentials.

Report concerns to the repository maintainer through GitHub, requesting a private contact channel without publishing personal details. The maintainer may remove inappropriate content, reject contributions, or restrict participation to keep the community safe and productive.
